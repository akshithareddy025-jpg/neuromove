ProblemResearch prototype

Why motor-imagery EEG is hard to classify

The signal carries intended movement — but it is noisy, variable, and hard to classify reliably.
Raw EEG
Noise & artifacts
Individual variability
Complex temporal patterns
Classification challenge
Why it matters
Accurate classification is a core building block of brain–computer interfaces — a channel that does not rely on muscles — and could support research into assistive human–machine interaction.
NeuroMove is a classification research prototype — no clinical, patient or hardware claims.
